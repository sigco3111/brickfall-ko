# Brickfall: brief (draft)

**The idea.** Demolition as a short, physical puzzle: one lot, one building, a clock, and every brick a real body that falls the way it should.

**What it's going for.**

- Collapses you can read and plan: hardness by material, a load meter, creaks and dust before a storey gives, so a good plan feels clever and a bad one is funny.
- Chain reactions worth chasing: propane, gas lines, charges caught in a blast, fire through timber, and a slow-motion replay of the best moment.
- Different ways to destroy: score chaos in Rush, drop it in its footprint in Blowdown, plan and run a heist in Salvage, or play freely in the Sandbox.
- Runs on the player's own GPU and tunes itself to it.

**What it isn't.** A game of models or a sandbox without goals: everything is voxels in code, and each lot is a contract with stars.
