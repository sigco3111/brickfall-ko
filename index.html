    This game needs WebGPU

    Every brick in Brickfall is a rigid body simulated on your graphics card through WebGPU. Without it there is nothing to knock down.

    It plays in:

    • Chrome or Edge 113 and later, on Windows, macOS and ChromeOS
    • Safari 26 on macOS and iOS
    • Firefox 141 and later, on Windows
